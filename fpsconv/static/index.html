No speed change. TrueHD / DTS-HD MA decode losslessly; anything lossy (AAC, DD+) is re-encoded — quality can only stay the same, never improve. Never upmixes: a 2.0 source stays 2.0. DDP 5.1 tops out at 1024 kbps; 1536 kbps is 7.1 only. Atmos needs a TrueHD Atmos source, truehdd and DEE 5.2.
Converted files are written here as name_mode.ext.
Audio has no fps of its own — pick the video it belongs to and the conversion is worked out per file.
DEE encodes are CPU-heavy; 2 is a good default.
